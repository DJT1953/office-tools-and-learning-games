PROOFREADER - HOW TO USE
========================

What it does
------------
Goes through Word (.doc, .docx), .rtf and .txt documents and fixes spelling,
grammar, punctuation, spacing and capital letters. It never changes your
originals: corrected copies go into a folder called "corrected" beside them.

One press of Start does it all. Each document is run automatically through
two checking programs, one after the other, in a single go:
  1. the free checker (LanguageTool), then
  2. Microsoft Word's own checker.
You do not start them separately or open either program yourself.

The app also works on a computer without Microsoft Word. It checks for Word
when it starts; if Word is not there, step 2 is skipped and the free checker
does the work alone. (Details under "On a computer without Microsoft Word".)

Swear words are never changed, flagged or softened.


Installing it (once)
--------------------
Double-click "Install_Proofreader.bat". It installs everything the
Proofreader needs except Microsoft Word, and asks before installing Python:

  - Python, only if the computer does not have it   https://www.python.org/
  - the Python add-ons PySide6, comtypes and python-docx
  - the free checker LanguageTool                   https://languagetool.org/
    with its own copy of Java (Eclipse Temurin)     https://adoptium.net/
    together about 300 MB to download
  - a "Proofreader" shortcut on the Desktop

It needs the internet and takes a few minutes. Running it again is safe.
Microsoft Word is optional and is not installed.

Needs Windows 10 or 11.


Starting it
-----------
Double-click the "Proofreader" shortcut on the Desktop, or
"Run_Proofreader.bat". You can also drag documents or folders onto either.

If you skipped the installer, the first press of Start asks to download the
free checker (about 300 MB). Either way this happens once. After that no
internet is needed and nothing you write leaves this computer.


Using it
--------
1. Add documents:  "Add files...", "Add a folder...", or drag them in.
   A folder means the documents directly in it. Tick "Include the folders
   inside any folder I add" to go deeper.
2. Tick what to fix.
3. Press "Start proofreading". "Stop" finishes the document in hand and stops.
4. Read the report in the lower half of the window. It is also saved in the
   "corrected" folder as "Proofreading report <date and time>.txt".


How it decides
--------------
The free checker goes first, then Microsoft Word's checker.

- A fix is made only when it is clear: one sensible answer, or both checkers
  agree on the same one.
- Anything doubtful is NOT changed. It is listed in the report under
  "LEFT FOR YOU TO DECIDE", with the likely choices.
- Names (capitalized words in the middle of a sentence), words in ALL CAPITALS
  and words with numbers in them are left alone.
- Short labels, such as table cells and list items, are not given a capital.

A document with nothing to correct gets no copy.
An earlier corrected copy is never written over; the new one is named
"name (2)", "name (3)" and so on.


Run-on sentences
----------------
Rewriting a run-on properly takes judgment a rule-based checker does not have.
So the app:
- fixes the clear case: two sentences joined with ", however" (also therefore,
  moreover, otherwise and similar words) get a semicolon;
- adds commas the checker is sure about;
- LISTS the rest in the report: very long sentences (over 45 words), sentences
  that look like two joined by a comma, and very long paragraphs.


The other tick boxes
--------------------
"Have Microsoft Word check after the free checker"
    Word settles spellings the free checker is unsure of, and the sentences
    Word's grammar check questions are listed in the report. Word does not
    offer its grammar fixes to other programs, so those are listed only.

"Also save a copy that shows each change"
    Adds "<name> - changes shown.docx" to the corrected folder. Open it in
    Word and use Review > Accept or Reject to go through the changes one by
    one. (Word documents only, not .txt.)

"Words to leave alone..."
    Names and special terms that must never be changed. One per line.


On a computer without Microsoft Word
------------------------------------
The app looks for Word when it starts. If Word is not there:
- Word's check is skipped, and the two tick boxes that need Word are greyed
  out, with a note saying so. The report says so too.
- .docx and .txt documents are still corrected, by the free checker alone.
  Expect a few more items under "LEFT FOR YOU TO DECIDE", since Word is not
  there to settle doubtful spellings.
- .doc and .rtf documents cannot be opened without Word. They are listed in
  the report as not corrected. Saved as .docx, they can be.
- In .docx files, footnotes and endnotes are not checked without Word.


Good to know
------------
- Fonts, bold, tables, headers, footers and footnotes keep their look. Only
  the words change.
- A document with tracked changes still waiting is skipped; accept or reject
  them in Word first. Protected and read-only documents are skipped too.
- The checkers do not catch everything. A wrong word that is spelled right
  ("are office" for "our office") can slip through. Skim the result.
- English (United States) only.


Files
-----
Install_Proofreader.bat  one-time setup
proofreader_setup.py     what the setup runs
Run_Proofreader.bat      starts the app
proofreader.py           the window
proofreader_engine.py    the rules for what to change and what to list
proofreader_tools.py     downloads and runs the free checker
proofreader_word.py      the Microsoft Word part
proofreader_docx.py      corrects .docx files when there is no Word
proofreader_job.py       copies, batches and the report

The free checker is stored in  %LOCALAPPDATA%\Proofreader\tools
Settings are stored in         %APPDATA%\Proofreader\settings.json
