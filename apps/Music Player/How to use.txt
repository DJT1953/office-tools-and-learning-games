MUSIC PLAYER - how to use it
============================

WHAT IT DOES
  Plays the music on this computer, internet radio, and Spotify, with big
  buttons and big words.
  - MY MUSIC: open a folder and every song in it and its subfolders plays.
    Your last 4 folders stay as buttons. Search the list, sort it, shuffle,
    repeat, change the speed, and save or open playlists. The list and the
    place in the song are remembered for next time.
  - RADIO: search about 50,000 free stations by name, type of music or
    country. The last hour of a station is kept, so live radio can be paused,
    sent back, taken back to the start of a song, or a marked part repeated.
  - SPOTIFY: open the Spotify app from the player (or a saved playlist link);
    the player's buttons, volume and Mute then work Spotify.
  - SLEEP TIMER: stops the music after a while or at the end of the song,
    and can then close the player, put the computer to sleep, or shut it
    down, after a 30-second warning you can cancel.

START IT
  Double-click Run_MusicPlayer.bat. Songs, folders or playlists dragged onto
  it play in it. For a desktop shortcut: right-click Run_MusicPlayer.bat,
  then Show more options, Send to, Desktop (create shortcut).
  Help > Keys and tips in the player lists the keyboard keys.

WHAT IT NEEDS
  1. Windows 10 or 11.

  2. Python 3.10 or newer (tested with Python 3.14). It is free:
       https://www.python.org/downloads/windows/
     The player starts with Python's "pyw" command, which the python.org
     installer sets up.

  3. Free add-ons. The first time the player starts, it lists any that are
     missing and offers to download and install them for you (about 190 MB,
     less if the Video Player or Video Editor is already set up). This needs
     an internet connection once.
       PySide6 - plays the music and draws the window
         https://pypi.org/project/PySide6/
       Mutagen - reads song names, artists and cover pictures
         https://pypi.org/project/mutagen/
       imageio-ffmpeg (FFmpeg) - brings in radio stations
         https://pypi.org/project/imageio-ffmpeg/
       winrt-Windows.Media.Control and winrt-Windows.Media - work Spotify's
       buttons and show its song
         https://pypi.org/project/winrt-Windows.Media.Control/
         https://pypi.org/project/winrt-Windows.Media/
       pycaw - sets Spotify's volume
         https://pypi.org/project/pycaw/
     To install them yourself instead, open Command Prompt and type:
       py -m pip install "pyside6>=6.10" mutagen imageio-ffmpeg winrt-Windows.Media.Control winrt-Windows.Media pycaw

  4. For radio: an internet connection. The station list comes from Radio
     Browser, a free list kept by volunteers:
       https://www.radio-browser.info/

  5. For Spotify (only if you want it): the Spotify app for Windows and a
     Spotify account, free or Premium:
       https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9ncbcszsjrsb  (Microsoft Store)
       https://www.spotify.com/download/windows/
     Spotify's buttons, song name and volume start working once Spotify has
     played something since it was opened.

GOOD TO KNOW
  - Your settings, your last list and the song details it has read are kept
    in the folder %APPDATA%\Music Player.
  - Sleep timer: on computers with no ordinary sleep (many newer laptops),
    "Put the computer to sleep" hibernates instead. The player says so. The
    computer turns off with everything kept, and the power button brings it
    back as it was.
  - Your music files are never changed, moved or deleted. Remove from list
    and Clear the list only take songs off the player's list.
