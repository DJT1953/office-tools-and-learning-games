DATING PHOTOGRAPHS - how to use it
==================================

WHAT IT DOES
  Reads the dates written on your old photos - on the front, the white border,
  or the back - and renames each photo by its date, like 1998-10-01.jpg, in a
  folder called "Dated Photographs" inside the folder you pick. Sorted by name,
  your photos are then in date order. It can also print the date on each
  photo in orange, the way a film camera did.
  It reads handwriting as well as print, and the orange date a film camera
  prints in the corner. Everything happens on this computer: nothing is uploaded.

  How dates are understood:
    Oct-98, 98-Oct, Oct '98, October 1998 ...... 1998-10-01 (no day: the 1st)
    June 12, 1975 / 12 June 1975 / 6/12/75 ..... 1975-06-12
    '98 10 12 (a camera's printed date) ........ 1998-10-12
    Christmas 1987 / Easter 1979 ............... 1987-12-25 / 1979-04-15
    Summer '82 ................................. 1982-07-01 (Spring Apr, Fall Oct, Winter Jan)
    1998 or '98 alone .......................... 1998-01-01
  Two-digit years after this year's (like '27) are taken as the 1900s.

START IT
  Double-click Run_DatingPhotos.bat (or a shortcut to it).

DATE YOUR PHOTOS
  1. Click "Pick a folder" and pick the folder of photos.
     - "Include photos in sub-folders" takes the folders inside it too.
     - If you scanned the BACKS of the photos as their own files, name them
       like their fronts (IMG_0001.jpg and IMG_0001_b.jpg, or
       IMG_0001 back.jpg) and they are found by themselves. If instead you
       scanned the front, then the back, then the next front, tick "Every
       second photo is the back of the one before it".
  2. Click "Find the dates". Each photo takes a few seconds. "Stop" stops
     after the photo being read; clicking "Find the dates" again carries on.
     You can start checking the photos already read while you wait.
  3. Check the dates. Click a photo in the list to see it on the right, with a
     red box round the words its date came from (the back shows beside the
     front). Then:
       - to change the date, type it in the Date box (1998-10-01, Oct 98 or
         1998 all work) and click "Use this date" or press Enter;
       - "Other dates found" lists any other dates in the writing;
       - "Skip this photo" leaves it where it is.
     Only ticked photos are moved. Click a tick to untick it.
     Dates in orange say "check this": the writing was hard to read, so they
     start unticked. Photos in red had no date found.
  4. Pick what to do with the dates, then click the big button (its words
     follow your choices: "Rename and move", "Print dates and move" or
     "Rename, print dates, move"). It asks first, then moves the ticked photos
     into "Dated Photographs". Photos without a date stay where they are.
       - "Rename each photo by its date" (on to start with): 1998-10-01.jpg.
         A photo's back goes with it (1998-10-01_b.jpg). Two photos from the
         same day become 1998-10-01.jpg and 1998-10-01 (2).jpg.
         "Keep the old name after the date" makes it 1998-10-01 IMG_0001.jpg.
       - "Print the date on each photo in orange, like a film camera": prints
         the date in the bottom-right corner, sized to the photo and inside any
         white border. Pick how it looks: '98 10 12, 10 12 '98 or 1998-10-12.
         Only what is known is printed: a photo dated "Oct-98" gets '98 10.
         The photo on the right shows how it will look before you move it.
         An untouched copy of each photo is kept in Dated Photographs\
         "Originals (no printed date)", and Undo puts that copy back.
         (JPG, PNG, TIFF, BMP and WebP only; not on backs.)
       - "Set each photo's Date taken to its date" (on to start with): writes
         the date into the photo's information, so Windows Photos and other
         photo programs file it under the right year. A scan otherwise carries
         the day it was scanned. The picture itself is not touched by this.
       - "Put them in a folder for each year": Dated Photographs\1998\...

  Changed your mind? "Undo the last move" puts the last batch back, with their
  old names and dates. "Open Dated Photographs" shows the folder.
  Every move is listed in Dated Photographs\Dating Photographs log.csv,
  which opens in Excel. Close it in Excel before moving or undoing: while it
  is open, the program can't add to the list, so it stops and tells you.
  To date more photos later, pick the folder your photos are in, not
  "Dated Photographs" itself.

FOR GOOD RESULTS
  - Scan at 300 dpi or more. Faint pencil reads better on a darker scan.
  - JPG, PNG, TIFF, BMP, GIF and WebP pictures all work. iPhone HEIC photos
    can't be opened: save them as JPG first.
  - Handwriting can be misread: check the dates before moving, especially the
    orange "check this" ones.

WHAT IT NEEDS
  1. Windows 10 or 11.
  2. Python 3.10 or newer (tested with 3.14), from
     https://www.python.org/downloads/windows/
  3. These free add-ons. On first start Dating Photographs offers to download
     and install them, with the handwriting reader's data (about 120 MB, once;
     needs an internet connection):
       Pillow ....... https://pypi.org/project/pillow/
       RapidOCR ..... https://pypi.org/project/rapidocr/ (reads handwriting)
       onnxruntime .. https://pypi.org/project/onnxruntime/
       pytesseract .. https://pypi.org/project/pytesseract/
  4. Optional: Tesseract OCR, which helps with printed words:
       https://github.com/UB-Mannheim/tesseract/wiki
  The Setup version (Dating Photographs Setup.exe) has everything inside it
  and needs nothing else.
