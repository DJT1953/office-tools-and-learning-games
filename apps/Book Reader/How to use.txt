BOOK READER - HOW TO USE

Start it by double-clicking Run_BookReader.bat. You can also drag book files
onto Run_BookReader.bat, or onto the open window, to open them.

It reads EPUB, Kindle (MOBI, AZW, AZW3, PRC), PDF, plain text (TXT) and saved
web pages (HTM, HTML, MHT). It can't open locked books (see the end).


THE LIBRARY

The first time, it looks through Documents\My Kindle Content. This can take a
minute; the line at the bottom counts the books as it goes.

- Click a book's cover to pick it. A row of buttons appears at the bottom:
  Open, Favorite, Finished, Collections, Save a copy, Show in folder and
  Remove from library. Double-clicking a cover opens the book too.
- Continue reading opens the book you read last, where you left off.
- Add books adds single book files. Add folder adds a whole folder of books.
- Type in the box at the top to find a book by its title or author.
- Order: changes the order of the covers (Most recent, Title, Author,
  Date added).
- All, Reading, Not started, Finished, Favorites and Locked show only those
  books. Collections shows one of your own collections.
- To make a collection, pick a book, press Collections, then
  New collection... and type its name.
- Remove from library only takes the book off the list. The file itself stays
  where it is.


READING

- Next page and Previous page are under the page. You can also click the left
  or right edge of the page, use the arrow keys or Page Up / Page Down, or turn
  the mouse wheel.
- The line under the page says the chapter, the page, how much of the book you
  have read, and about how long the chapter will take. Drag the slider to jump
  anywhere in the book.
- The book always reopens where you stopped.
- If part of a book can't be shown, it says so and goes back to the library.

Buttons at the top:
- Library: back to all your books.
- Contents: the list of chapters. Click one to go there.
- Search: type words and press Find. Click a result to go there; the words
  are marked in orange.
- Bookmark: marks the page. Press it again (Remove bookmark) to take the
  mark off.
- Notes: your bookmarks and highlights, with your notes. Click one to go
  there. Save notes to a file writes them into a text file.
- Text: text size (Smaller / Bigger), font, colours (White, Sepia, Night),
  One page, Two pages side by side, or Scroll, Space between lines (Close,
  Normal, Wide), Margins, and Straight right-hand edge.
- Read aloud: reads the book out loud with a Windows voice and turns the pages
  by itself. While it reads, a bar has Pause (then Go on), Stop, Slower,
  Faster and Voice. Press Read aloud (now called Stop reading) again to stop.
- Full screen: the page fills the screen. Press Exit full screen or the Esc
  key to come back.
- Save a copy: saves the book as EPUB, PDF, Word or plain text.

Picking words: hold the left mouse button down and drag across some words. A
bar appears with Yellow, Green, Blue and Pink (to highlight them in that
colour), Add note, Copy, Look up, Read aloud from here, and Cancel.
- Look up asks a free dictionary on the internet what the word means. Only the
  first word you picked is sent. Look it up on the web opens Wiktionary in your
  web browser.
- Click a highlight to change its colour, or press Edit note or Remove.

Links in a book: footnote and chapter links go there, and a Back button
appears under the page to take you back. A link to a web page asks before
opening your web browser. Click a picture to see it bigger.

Keys: Ctrl+F searches, Ctrl+B bookmarks, F11 full screen, Esc closes a bar or
leaves full screen.


PDFs

PDF pages are fixed, so their words can't be made bigger or recoloured, and
words can't be highlighted. Instead there are buttons under the page: Zoom out,
Zoom in, Fit width, Whole page and Continuous pages. Bookmarks, Contents (when
the PDF has them), Search and Read aloud work. A PDF that needs a password asks
for it.


LOCKED BOOKS

Many books bought from Amazon are locked with copy protection. They show a
padlock on the cover and "Locked by Amazon" and open only in Amazon's Kindle app or at read.amazon.com.
Books locked by Samsung Books open only in the Samsung Books app; when they
are free Project Gutenberg books, Open offers the page where you can download
a free, unlocked copy. The Book Reader never removes copy protection.


WHAT IT NEEDS
  1. Windows 10 or 11.

  2. Python 3.10 or newer (tested with Python 3.14). It is free:
       https://www.python.org/downloads/windows/
     The Book Reader starts with Python's "pyw" command, which the python.org
     installer sets up.

  3. Free add-ons. The first time the Book Reader starts, it lists any that
     are missing and offers to download and install them for you (about
     200 MB, less if the Music Player, Video Player or Video Editor is
     already set up). This needs an internet connection once.
       PySide6 - shows the books, the PDFs and the window, and reads aloud
         https://pypi.org/project/PySide6/
       lxml - reads EPUB books
         https://pypi.org/project/lxml/
       comtypes - lets Microsoft Word save copies as Word documents
         https://pypi.org/project/comtypes/
     To install them yourself instead, open Command Prompt and type:
       py -m pip install "pyside6>=6.10" lxml comtypes

  4. For Look up: an internet connection. Meanings come from the free
     dictionary at:
       https://dictionaryapi.dev/
     Look it up on the web opens Wiktionary:
       https://en.wiktionary.org/

  5. For Save a copy as Word (only if you want it): Microsoft Word.
       https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/word

  6. For Read aloud: the voices that come with Windows. More can be added in
     Windows Settings, under Time & language, then Speech.

GOOD TO KNOW
  - Your places, bookmarks, highlights and settings are kept in the folder
    %APPDATA%\Book Reader.
  - Your book files are never changed, moved or deleted. Remove from library
    only takes a book off the list.
