SONG CLEANER
============

Tidies a folder of songs recorded from the radio: better names, copies of
the same song removed, and talk or noise trimmed from the ends.

THE FIRST TIME

   Double-click  Install_SongCleaner.bat  once. It installs everything the
   Song Cleaner needs, all free:
     - Python, if this computer does not have it
       (https://www.python.org/downloads/)
     - the Python add-ons PySide6, numpy, mutagen and imageio-ffmpeg
       (about 300 MB to download, 850 MB installed; imageio-ffmpeg brings
       FFmpeg, which reads and cuts MP3s)
     - if you say yes, the small song recogniser Chromaprint fpcalc
       (https://acoustid.org/chromaprint), only needed to look names up online
     - a "Song Cleaner" shortcut on the Desktop
   Running it again is safe.

After that, start it from the Desktop shortcut or by double-clicking
Run_SongCleaner.bat


THE THREE STEPS

1. Scan
   Press "Choose folder" and pick the folder your songs are in, then press
   "1. Scan". It reads every MP3. Nothing is changed. The first scan of a
   big folder takes a while (about 20 minutes for 5,000 songs); after that
   it remembers each song, so later scans take seconds. You can press Stop
   and carry on another day.

2. Look through the pages
   Every proposal has a tick box. Untick anything you do not want.
   The Play buttons let you hear a song, or just the part that would be cut.

3. Apply
   Press "3. Apply ticked changes". It tells you what it is about to do and
   asks first. "Undo last Apply" puts everything back.


NOTHING IS DELETED

   Copies that are removed go to a folder beside your music folder:
        Song Cleaner - removed copies
   The untouched originals of trimmed songs go to:
        Song Cleaner - originals before trimming
   When you are happy with the result you can delete those two folders
   yourself to get the disk space back. Until then, nothing is lost.


THE PAGES

Names
   The name now and the name proposed. It removes things like "Auto Dj Bud"
   and "_01", puts "abba-waterloo" right, finishes titles that were cut
   off, and makes one artist's spellings agree. "Edit name" lets you type
   your own. A doubtful name (no title, or a record label where the title
   should be) is listed with no change.

Copies
   Files that are the same recording, found by their sound, not their
   names. One is marked KEEP: the fullest, then the one with least talk,
   then the best sound. The others are ticked for removal. "Keep this one
   instead" changes which. Where the program cannot tell which copy is the
   fuller, nothing is ticked, so listen and choose.

Trims
   Cuts the program is sure of. It is only sure when two signs agree: a gap
   in the sound, a change in the sound, or another copy of the same song.

Listen and decide
   Cuts it is not sure of. Very often what one recording has "extra" is
   really more of the song, which the other station faded early. Play
   them, and tick only the ones you want. "Start earlier", "Start later",
   "End earlier" and "End later" move a cut by half a second.

Problems
   Files that could not be read, songs that share a name but are different
   recordings (both are kept), and anything Apply had to skip.


LOOKING NAMES UP ONLINE (optional)

   For doubtful names the program can ask AcoustID, a free public music
   database. It needs a free key. Press Settings for the steps. The first
   time, it also asks to download a small free program that recognises
   songs. Only the song's fingerprint and length are sent, never the file.


GOOD TO KNOW

   Trimming does not lower the sound quality. The song is not re-recorded;
   the unwanted part is simply left off.
   A cut cannot be faded, so it is placed on the quietest moment nearby.
   A DJ talking over the music cannot be removed, only cut off together
   with that part of the song.
   The program expects one song per file, as MP3.
