  • Video Editor

    Trims, splits, inserts and splices videos and sound files, with volume, fades and added music or narration, then saves in the format and folder you choose.

    Needs Python 3. Downloads about 190 MB of add-ons the first time (40 MB if you already have the Video Player).

    Memory warning: saving long 4K edits needs a lot of memory, about 5 GB for 20 pieces of 4K video (HD needs far less). On a PC with 8 GB or less, keep 4K edits short or save them in parts.

    Download

  • Music Player

    Plays the music on this PC, internet radio with pause and rewind, and Spotify, with big buttons and a sleep timer.

    Needs Windows 10 or 11 and Python 3.10 or newer. Downloads about 190 MB of free add-ons the first time (PySide6, Mutagen, imageio-ffmpeg, winrt-Windows.Media.Control, winrt-Windows.Media, pycaw).

    Radio needs the internet (stations from Radio Browser). Spotify is optional: it needs the Spotify app and a free or Premium account.

    Download

  • Book Reader

    Reads EPUB, Kindle (MOBI, AZW, AZW3, PRC), PDF and text books with big buttons: a library of covers sorted by title, author or genre, links to free books, six page colours and a dimmer for night reading, contents, search, bookmarks, highlights and notes, look up, read aloud, and copies as EPUB, PDF, Word or text.

    Needs Windows 10 or 11 and Python 3.10 or newer. Downloads about 200 MB of free add-ons the first time (PySide6, lxml, comtypes).

    Look up needs the internet (meanings from the Free Dictionary API). Saving a copy as Word needs Microsoft Word. Free books opens Project Gutenberg or Standard Ebooks: free books, no account needed. Locked books (most bought from Amazon) are listed but never unlocked.

    Download

  • Proofreader

    Fixes spelling, grammar, punctuation, spacing and capital letters in batches of Word (.doc, .docx), .rtf and .txt documents, or whole folders. One press of Start runs each document through two checkers, one after the other: the free LanguageTool, then Microsoft Word's own. Corrected copies and a report of every change are saved beside the originals, doubtful fixes are listed for you instead of guessed, and swear words are never changed. Works without Microsoft Word too.

    Needs Windows 10 or 11. Install_Proofreader.bat installs everything else, asking before it installs Python 3.10 or newer: the free add-ons PySide6, comtypes and python-docx, and the free checker LanguageTool with its own Java; about 500 MB in all.

    Microsoft Word is optional: without it, .docx and .txt documents are still corrected; .doc and .rtf need Word. English (United States) only.

    Download

  • Song Cleaner

    Tidies a folder of MP3 songs recorded from the radio: fixes their names (DJ tags, copy numbers, cut-off titles, odd spellings), finds copies of the same recording by their sound and keeps the fullest, and trims silence, talk and noise from the ends without lowering the sound quality. Every change is shown first with Play buttons, nothing is deleted, and Undo puts it all back.

    Needs Windows 10 or 11. Install_SongCleaner.bat installs everything else, asking before it installs Python 3.10 or newer: the free add-ons PySide6, numpy, mutagen and imageio-ffmpeg with FFmpeg; about 300 MB in all.

    Optional: to look up doubtful names online, the free Chromaprint fpcalc (the installer asks) and a free AcoustID key. MP3 files only.

    Download
