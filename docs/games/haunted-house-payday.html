Nothing pays by itself: the player clicks the green hand when they think they have counted enough. Clicking it too soon gets "Not yet." Adding a bill past the price counts on into red squares, then the bill goes back with a gentle correction.

Each lit square on the counting road is one dollar. The player never adds or subtracts: every bill is counted one dot at a time, carrying on from the squares already lit. When they hand over a bill that is bigger than they need, change comes back counted up from the price, the way an honest cashier does it. On levels 4 and 5 a monster sometimes holds back a bill, and the player has to spot the empty blue squares.

Monsters speak with the computer's plain built-in voices, pitched up or down, because the smoother "Natural" voices mostly ignore pitch. On Windows that usually means David for most monsters and Zira for the Witch. If a monster is hard to understand, switch Monster voices to the narrator.

Progress is saved in this browser only. Back up progress gives you a code to keep somewhere safe; Restore from backup puts it back, on this computer or another one.
