Each level has ten two- and three-syllable words. A first-try match lights a green note, a helped match a gold one. The carols are traditional and public domain, played from the melodies printed on Wikipedia; each plays its first verse and chorus. Progress is saved in this browser on this computer only; Back up progress gives you a code to keep it safe.
