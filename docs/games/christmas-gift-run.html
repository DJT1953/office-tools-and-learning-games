Progress

The player never adds or subtracts. The meter has five boxes, one per dollar. Each small square is 5 cents, so a dollar bill fills a whole box, a quarter fills one row, a dime fills 2 squares and a nickel fills 1. The price is the square with the red bow. The player fills squares up to the bow, and the voice says the running total after every coin.

Nothing pays by itself: the player clicks the green hand when they think they have enough. Clicking too soon gets "Not yet." Adding a coin past the price lights red squares, then the coin goes back with a gentle correction. When they hand over more than the price, the robot counts the change back up from the price, the way an honest cashier does. On level 5 a robot sometimes holds back a coin, and the player has to spot the empty blue squares.

There are no pennies, because the U.S. stopped making them in 2025 and stores now round cash to the nearest nickel. Robots speak with the computer's plain built-in voices, pitched up or down. If one is hard to understand, switch robot voices to the narrator.

Progress is saved in this browser only. Back up progress gives you a code to keep somewhere safe; Restore from backup puts it back, on this computer or another one.
